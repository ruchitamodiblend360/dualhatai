How it works

From input to insight in seconds

Two tools, one goal — give your team the clarity they need to ship with confidence.

1
Readiness Checker

Paste your user story

Drop in any user story or epic — rough draft, ticket copy, or work-in-progress. Optionally add a parent epic and your team's Definition of Ready for a more tailored score.

2
Readiness Checker

Get an instant AI readiness score

The AI evaluates the story across five dimensions — completeness, clarity, testability, size, and dependency risk — and returns a score out of 100 with specific gap callouts and a rewritten improved version.

3
Status Generator

Connect your Jira board

Link your Jira workspace once. DualHat AI pulls your boards, sprints, and backlog automatically — no copy-pasting, no manual data entry.

4
Status Generator

Generate an executive status deck

Pick a sprint or backlog row and hit Generate. The AI synthesises accomplishments, next steps, blockers, and sprint metrics into a Blend-branded PowerPoint deck — ready to present in under 30 seconds.

Features

Everything a PM needs, nothing they don't

Built specifically for delivery teams — fast, focused, and connected to the tools you already use.

Readiness Checker

AI-powered readiness score

Score any story out of 100 across five dimensions: completeness, clarity, testability, size fit, and dependency risk — in under 3 seconds.

Readiness Checker

Instant story rewrite

Every analysis includes an AI-improved version of your story — better structured, clearer ACs, ready to paste straight back into Jira.

Readiness Checker

Definition of Ready scoring

Add your team's DoR and the score adapts to your bar — not a generic checklist. Works with any DoR format.

Readiness Checker

Analysis history & dashboard

Every story you check is saved to your session dashboard. Review past scores, re-run analyses, and track how stories improve over time.

Status Generator

Live Jira integration

Connects directly to your Jira workspace. Reads boards, sprints, and issue data in real time — no exports, no CSVs, no manual sync.

Status Generator

One-click status deck

Select a sprint or backlog row and generate a complete executive status PowerPoint — accomplishments, next steps, blockers, and metrics — in under 30 seconds.

Status Generator

Blend-branded PPTX output

Every generated deck uses the official Blend colour palette, Montserrat typography, and the Blend logo — brand-ready without any design work.
